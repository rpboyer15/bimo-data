# Recorded pronunciations

Twelve of Bimo's word pronunciations are recordings of a person rather
than the synthetic voice: 也, 今天, 你, 写, 多少, 我, 找, 没有, 给, 请, 那些, 面条儿.

They come from Yue Tan's *Collection audio libre de mots chinois
(mandarins)*, recorded at the Université de Caen for the Shtooka project,
Copyright (c) 2009 Yue Tan, licensed under
[CC BY-SA 3.0 United States](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/us/),
and obtained through the mirror at github.com/hugolpz/audio-cmn.

Bimo changed them: silence trimmed from both ends, the level brought down
to match the app's other files, and converted to 24 kHz mono MP3. These
changed files are licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](../licenses/CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt),
which the original license's share-alike clause allows.
