# Bimo support

Bimo (笔墨) is an offline app for learning to read and write Chinese
characters, made for the iPad with an Apple Pencil and also available on
the iPhone.

## Contact

Write to bimo.support@gmail.com with questions, problems, or a wrong
definition, reading, pronunciation or hint. Please say which word or
character, and which device you use.

## Common questions

**My progress did not appear on my other device.** Both devices must be
signed in to the same iCloud account with iCloud Drive on, and Sync with
iCloud must be on in Bimo's Settings on each. Progress arrives within a
few minutes of opening the app; the Settings screen shows the last sync.

**A finger does not write after I used the Pencil.** Once a Pencil has
written, Bimo treats a finger as a resting palm. Turn on Allow finger
writing in Settings to write with a finger.

**How do I start again?** Settings, Reset all progress. It deletes the
progress on every device signed in to your iCloud account and keeps your
own memory tips and settings.

**Is the voice a real person?** Most pronunciations are a synthetic voice
made with Microsoft Azure AI Speech; a few words are recordings of a
person, credited in the app under About.

## Privacy

Bimo has no accounts, analytics or advertising and sends nothing to
anyone. The full policy is in [privacy](privacy.md).
