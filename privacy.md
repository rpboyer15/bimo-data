# Bimo privacy policy

Bimo keeps your study data on your iPad or iPhone. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and sends nothing to us or to anyone else.

Sync with iCloud is on unless you turn it off in Settings. While it is on, your progress, memory tips and study settings are stored in your own iCloud account through Apple's CloudKit and iCloud key-value storage, under Apple's terms, and Bimo cannot read them. With sync off, everything stays on the device.

If you turn on the daily reminder, Bimo schedules notifications on the device itself. Nothing leaves the device for that.

The pronunciations were generated once, before the app was published, with Microsoft's text-to-speech service. The app does not contact that service or any other server while you use it.

Deleting the app deletes its data from the device. Data in iCloud can be removed in the device's iCloud settings under Manage Storage.

Questions go to bimo.support@gmail.com.
